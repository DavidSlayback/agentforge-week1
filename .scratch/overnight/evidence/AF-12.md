# AF-12 evidence — synthetic load and cost measurement

**Status:** `MEASURED_LOCAL_SYNTHETIC_SIGNED_MODE`

The checked-in harness is `scripts/agentforge-load.mjs`. It exercises the
briefing HTTP contract at configurable concurrency and records p50/p95/p99,
throughput, error rate, workload mode, and the post-run metrics snapshot.

Required runs:

```text
node scripts/agentforge-load.mjs --base-url <candidate-url> --concurrency 10 --requests 100 --out .scratch/overnight/evidence/load-10.json
node scripts/agentforge-load.mjs --base-url <candidate-url> --concurrency 50 --requests 500 --out .scratch/overnight/evidence/load-50.json
```

No scale or cost claim is closed until both reports exist and their environment,
resource, timestamp, and provider-mode assumptions are reviewed. The current
deterministic provider reports zero provider spend; no hosted-vendor price is
invented.
