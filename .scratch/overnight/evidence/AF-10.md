# AF-10 evidence — provider-ready failure lane

**Status:** `IMPLEMENTED_PROVIDER_READY_SYNTHETIC_ONLY`

## Scope

The deterministic provider remains the default. An optional OpenAI-compatible
provider is wrapped by `ResilientProvider`, which retries twice and then uses a
deterministic fallback. No approved vendor is selected and no PHI may enter a
provider path.

latency, provider terms, cost, or suitability for clinical data. Owner review is
required before any approved-vendor experiment.
