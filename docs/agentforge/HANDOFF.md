If unavailable, use the most recent completed PCP visit and label it accurately; without a PCP baseline, show dated history without an invented comparison.
  - Conflicts: show unresolved disagreements with sources. Show a later explicitly documented resolution as resolved, retaining provenance. Recency or omission alone is not resolution.
  - Interaction: embedded patient-chart panel with a "Prepare for visit" action, visible active patient, and follow-up conversation. Citation inspection shows excerpt, record type, author when available, exact date, and route to original record.
  - Missing data: empty successful lookup, no prior visit, and retrieval failure are distinct. Partial retrieval qualifies the summary as incomplete, identifies unverifiable coverage, withholds dependent conclusions, and offers retry. Failed tool/API/record-loading calls are not empty results.
  - Authorization: no patient briefing or evidence if identity/access cannot be established. Existing login may recover expired authentication, followed by a fresh permission check; authenticated denial remains denial.
  - Patient switching: preserve previous conversations for return, but isolate conversations and active patient context; fresh authorization is required. No cross-patient context mixing.
  - Success intent: compact paragraph or two, minimal visual clutter, efficient summarization, many useful hyperlinks, no missed relevant records/changes, no unsupported claims, no unauthorized interactions, and no incorrect discrepancy resolutions.
